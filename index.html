About Me

Curious by nature. Precise by practice.

I tend to start with questions.

What is actually broken? What do users really need? What are we assuming? And what needs to happen next?

From there, I turn ambiguity into structure: research, requirements, priorities, workflows, experiments, and eventually something shipped.

I've worked across product, operations, client delivery, and early-stage projects, often with very little instruction and a lot of figuring things out. I enjoy being close to the problem, talking to people, making sense of messy information, and keeping things moving until there is something real to show for it.
