CASE STUDY

VERDIQ

Validated and shipped a fintech platform MVP in four weeks.

Problem

There was no clear product direction for a fintech concept and the team needed a fast MVP with defined user value.

Approach

I structured the scope into core value flows, aligned stakeholder expectations, and mapped MVP delivery across four weeks.

Research

Conducted competitor analysis and persona mapping to focus on the highest-value product capabilities first.

Decisions

Prioritized launch-critical features, simplified the roadmap, and kept the sprint cycle tight to maintain momentum.

Execution

Managed the delivery process with Jira, coordinated engineering, and ensured each milestone had a working output.

Outcome

Delivered a live fintech product in four weeks, with clear progress reports and stakeholder control over scope.

Lessons Learned

Prioritizing clarity and fast feedback loops is the best way to ship early-stage product work without overbuilding.
