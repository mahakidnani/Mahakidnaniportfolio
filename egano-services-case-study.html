CASE STUDY

Egano Services

Delivered a client website with clear execution and iterative feedback.

Problem

The client needed a website but the requirements were not structured and the business goals were still forming.

Approach

I distilled the brief into a prioritized build plan, established review points, and aligned the website content with business outcomes.

Research

Reviewed competitor experiences, client audience expectations, and technical tradeoffs before selecting an implementation path.

Decisions

Chose a JavaScript web build with lightweight structure to enable faster iteration and clear stakeholder visibility.

Execution

Built the website, managed feedback rounds, and shipped a working site aligned to the client’s goals.

Outcome

Created a stakeholder-approved website that reduced delivery uncertainty and supported client follow-up conversations.

Lessons Learned

Structure and frequent alignment in early stages makes project delivery faster and more confident.
