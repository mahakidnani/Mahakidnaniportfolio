CASE STUDY

Lamatic.ai

Created an operating system for a startup team in one night.

Problem

The team lacked a clear operations framework, making onboarding, weekly rhythm, and tool usage inconsistent.

Approach

I researched the team, tools, and priorities, then built a compact onboarding and operations system for the startup.

Research

Reviewed company hires, stack, and priorities, and identified gaps in team coordination and handoff clarity.

Decisions

Focused on practical operating routines, concise team updates, and a simple SaaS tracker rather than a broad process document.

Execution

Built the operating system in Notion and shared a concise walkthrough so stakeholders could act immediately.

Outcome

Delivered a usable CoS system that reduced handoff friction and made planning more visible for the startup team.

Lessons Learned

Independent execution requires making the simplest useful recommendation and communicating it quickly.
