CASE STUDY

Clarity Point Consultancy

Structured delivery from a vague brief to a launch-ready website.

Problem

Client requirements were unclear and evolving, with no execution framework or handoff plan in place.

Approach

I mapped the brief into defined milestones, aligned stakeholder goals, and established a delivery rhythm.

Research

Reviewed client priorities, competitor sites, and user-facing messaging to validate the scope before building.

Decisions

Chose a WordPress build for faster launch, simplified the content structure, and kept the scope focused on conversion clarity.

Execution

Owned the project end to end, from client discovery through QA and final handoff, delivering the site on the agreed timeline.

Outcome

Delivered a polished website that aligned with client goals, reduced their launch risk, and enabled a faster approval cycle.

Lessons Learned

Early alignment on scope and review checkpoints removes ambiguity and makes it easier to ship independently.
